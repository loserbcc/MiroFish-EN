[BREAKING] Major Tech Giant Announces 15% Layoffs, Affecting ~12,000 Workers

According to sources familiar with the matter, global technology company TechNova announced today that it will conduct large-scale layoffs, cutting approximately 15% of its workforce — an estimated 12,000 employees.

TechNova CEO James Chen stated in an internal memo: "Facing macroeconomic uncertainty and the rapid transformation driven by AI technology, we must refocus on our core business and concentrate resources on artificial intelligence and cloud computing."

Following the announcement, TechNova stock rose 3.2% in after-hours trading, as investors welcomed the cost-cutting measures. However, on social media, numerous current and former TechNova employees expressed frustration and anxiety.

Industry analyst Sarah Li noted: "This is the third wave of major tech layoffs this year. Companies are shifting labor costs toward AI infrastructure investment — a trend that will continue over the next 12 months."

Union representative Maria Wang responded: "The company is laying off workers despite a 22% profit increase last year — this is unacceptable to employees. We will organize collective action to protect workers' rights."
